The KG Consumer uses information and insights from a knowledge graph to understand domain-specific relationships, assess findings, and make decisions. Their primary focus is the underlying domain question rather than the graph's technical structure.

KG Consumers primarily have expertise in the data domain, business context, overarching use case, or sociocultural context. They typically do not interact directly with the graph database or its query language. Instead, they rely on KG Analysts, graphical query interfaces, or automated reports to obtain relevant insights.
