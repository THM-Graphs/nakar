The KG Builder creates and maintains the technical and semantic foundation of a knowledge graph. They integrate source data, develop the schema, and ensure that the graph supports its intended applications.

KG Builders typically have expertise in database systems, data management, or data modeling. They select an appropriate database or representation method and are often proficient in the associated graph query languages. They work with Analysts to align the graph's structure with its intended analytical use cases.
