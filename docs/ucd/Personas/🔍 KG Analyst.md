The KG Analyst explores and analyzes a knowledge graph to discover relevant information, patterns, and relationships. They communicate their findings to other users or use them in downstream analysis and AI/ML tasks.

KG Analysts typically have expertise in data science or machine learning. They are familiar with extracting information through graph query languages but are not necessarily query-language experts. They often collaborate closely with KG Builders or perform both roles themselves.
